# Camplicity Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026
**Company:** Camplicity LLC, a Georgia limited liability company (“**Camplicity**,” “**we**,” “**us**”)
**Contact:** contact@camplicity.app

This Privacy Policy describes how Camplicity collects, uses, discloses, and retains personal information. It does not claim that Camplicity is certified under any particular privacy or security framework.

---

## Three relationships

1. **Website visitors and business contacts** — people who visit camplicity.app, request information, or communicate with us.
2. **Authorized Users** — adults who log in to a camp’s Camplicity workspace, such as directors and staff.
3. **Customer Data** — information a camp customer provides or imports about campers, staff, schedules, and operations.

For account administration, billing, security, support, Site analytics, and our own business records, Camplicity determines why and how information is processed and acts as a business or controller as applicable. For Customer Data, **Camplicity generally acts as the camp’s service provider or processor**. The camp decides what to provide and how to use it for camp operations.

Parents, campers, and staff seeking access, correction, or deletion of records held in a camp workspace should contact **the camp**. We will reasonably assist the camp as described in our Data Processing Addendum (“**DPA**”).

The **SaaS Terms** and DPA contain contractual obligations for the Services. This Policy provides notice of our privacy practices and does not reduce rights provided by applicable law.

---

## 1. Information we handle

**Website visitors and business contacts.** Name, email, organization, and message if you contact us; IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, referring pages, and approximate location derived from IP; and cookie or analytics information. Google Analytics may receive page-view and device information when it is enabled.

**Authorized Users.** Name, email, Auth0 authentication identifier, verification and last-login information, role and access scope, optional photo, tenant and subscription information, and support communications.

**Customer Data (processed for the camp).** Depending on what the camp enters or imports: camper and staff names, dates of birth, gender, staff phone numbers, photos, descriptions and notes, groups, sessions, housing, activities, trips, meals, availability, certifications, allergy and dietary labels, external-system identifiers, elective choices, schedules, and assignments. Free-text fields may contain other information chosen by the camp. We do not independently decide which people are entered. We do not currently offer camper or parent logins.

**Billing and onboarding.** Organization and camp names, administrator name and email, subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, and legal-document acceptance records such as the acceptance time and document versions. Stripe processes payment-card information under its own privacy notice; Camplicity does not store complete payment-card numbers.

**Support and security.** Support messages may include the submitting user’s name and email, page URL, browser information, and screenshots the user chooses to attach. We also process authentication, request, audit, import-job, and security logs, which may include user email, entity names, timestamps, IP address, and actions taken.

**Optional AI features.** If an Authorized User invokes an available AI feature, we process the user’s prompt, recent conversation context, relevant tool or draft information, generated output, and usage information. A prompt or context may contain Customer Data selected or entered by the user.

We collect information directly from users and customers, automatically from browsers and the Services, from authentication and payment providers, and from integrations the customer directs us to use.

## 2. How we use information

**Website, accounts, and business operations:** provide and secure the Site and accounts; authenticate and authorize users; administer subscriptions and collect fees; respond to inquiries and support requests; understand Site and product usage; maintain audit and business records; prevent abuse; enforce agreements; and comply with law.

**Customer Data as a service provider or processor:** host, organize, display, secure, support, and troubleshoot the Services; run imports, exports, and customer-directed integrations; process prompts when an Authorized User invokes an available AI feature; prevent misuse; comply with law; and create aggregated or de-identified information as permitted by the DPA.

We do **not** sell Customer Data, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to train general-purpose AI models.

---

## 3. Cookies and analytics

We use technologies needed for authentication, security, and sessions. When Google Analytics is configured, it receives page path, page URL, page title, and associated device or usage information under Google’s terms. We do not intentionally send camper records to analytics tools. Page URLs and titles should not be used to enter personal information.

Browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies. Blocking essential storage or cookies may prevent login or other features from working. Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential analytics, we will request it before using those technologies.

## 4. How we share information

- **Vendors that help us operate**, including Amazon Web Services (application hosting, database, storage, content delivery, secrets, and email infrastructure), Auth0/Okta (authentication), Stripe (billing and payments), Google Analytics when configured, GitHub for report text and metadata when a user submits an in-product support report (not screenshot files), and OpenAI when an Authorized User invokes an available AI feature.
- **The camp** that owns the workspace.
- **Customer-directed integrations.** CampMinder and another system a customer connects are generally the customer’s providers. The customer instructs us to exchange information with them.
- **Professional advisors, insurers, and authorities** as reasonably necessary to obtain advice, comply with law or legal process, enforce agreements, or protect rights, security, and safety.
- **Corporate-transaction recipients** in connection with a financing, merger, acquisition, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of assets, subject to appropriate confidentiality and notice where required.

Our DPA identifies the subprocessors used for Customer Data and describes how changes are handled.

## 5. Children

Camplicity is a business tool for camps. It is **not directed to children**, and children must not create accounts or submit information directly to us.

Camps may direct us to process information **about** children, including children under 13. The camp is responsible for providing notices and obtaining parental consent or other authority required for its collection and use. Parents should read the camp’s notices and contact the camp to access, correct, or delete camper information.

If we learn that a child created a login or submitted personal information directly in violation of these rules, we will take reasonable steps to close the login and delete the information, subject to legal retention requirements. Contact contact@camplicity.app if you believe this occurred.

---

## 6. Health and school records

Camplicity is not an electronic health record, medication-administration system, or emergency system and is not offered as HIPAA or FERPA certified. Allergy and dietary labels, disability-related information, or other health-adjacent information a camp chooses to enter may be sensitive personal information under applicable law. Do not use Camplicity as a HIPAA business associate or FERPA school-official system unless we have signed a separate agreement for that use.

Customers should minimize sensitive information, limit access by role, and avoid placing diagnoses, medication instructions, government identifiers, payment-card numbers, or detailed medical records in free-text fields.

## 7. Security and retention

We use administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information, including Auth0-based authentication, role-based permissions, tenant and camp scoping, protected cloud storage, encrypted transport for production services, encryption of stored integration credentials, logging, and access controls. These measures are described more specifically in the DPA and apply where relevant to the feature and environment.

No system is completely secure. We do not claim SOC 2 or ISO certification, HIPAA or FERPA compliance, a particular penetration-testing schedule, or encryption in every location unless we publish a specific attestation.

- Website inquiries and business communications: while needed to respond and for a reasonable business-record period afterward.
- Account, billing, and support data: for the account or customer relationship and afterward as needed for legitimate business, tax, fraud-prevention, and dispute purposes.
- Customer Data: during the subscription; after access ends, a 30-day export-request period and deletion or de-identification from active systems within 60 days, subject to legal retention and the backup exception in the SaaS Terms and DPA.
- Successful import-job files and records: ordinarily up to 30 days; failed import-job files and records: ordinarily up to 90 days.
- Audit, authentication, AI conversation, support, and security records: for as long as reasonably necessary for service operation, customer support, security, legal compliance, and disputes. Customers should avoid placing unnecessary personal information in prompts, screenshots, and free-text fields.

## 8. Your choices and rights

**Authorized Users:** ask your camp administrator, or email contact@camplicity.app for information Camplicity controls.

**Campers, parents, and staff:** contact the **camp** for information in its workspace. We will reasonably assist the camp under the DPA.

Depending on your state and our legal obligations, you may have rights to confirm processing; know, access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of information; and opt out of certain sale, sharing, targeted-advertising, or profiling activities. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

For information Camplicity controls, email contact@camplicity.app with “Privacy Request.” Describe your request and your relationship with us. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and authority, including authority to act as an authorized agent. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and may deny or limit a request where an exception applies. We will not discriminate against you for exercising an applicable right.

To appeal a denial where an appeal right applies, email contact@camplicity.app with “Privacy Appeal” and explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered. We will provide appeal instructions and any regulator contact required by applicable law.
