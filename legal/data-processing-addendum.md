# Camplicity Data Processing Addendum

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026
**Provider:** Camplicity LLC, a Georgia limited liability company (“**Camplicity**”)
**Contact:** contact@camplicity.app

This Data Processing Addendum (“**DPA**”) forms part of the Camplicity SaaS Terms or another written agreement that incorporates it (the “**Agreement**”) between Camplicity and the customer identified in the Agreement (“**Customer**”). It applies when Camplicity processes Customer Personal Data on Customer’s behalf in providing the Services.

By accepting the Agreement, each party accepts this DPA. Capitalized terms not defined here have the meanings given in the Agreement.

---

## 1. Definitions

**“Applicable Data Protection Law”** means a United States federal, state, or local privacy or data-protection law that applies to a party’s processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. It includes applicable provisions of comprehensive state privacy laws and the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (“**CCPA**”).

**“Controller”** includes a “business” under the CCPA and a person that determines the purposes and means of processing personal data under another Applicable Data Protection Law.

**“Customer Personal Data”** means personal information or personal data contained in Customer Data that Camplicity processes on Customer’s behalf. It does not include information for which Camplicity independently determines the purposes and means of processing, such as Camplicity’s own billing, corporate, and legal-compliance records.

**“Data Subject”** includes a consumer or other identified or identifiable individual described by Customer Personal Data.

**“Process”** and **“processing”** mean any operation performed on personal information or personal data, including collection, storage, organization, use, disclosure, transmission, deletion, or de-identification.

**“Processor”** includes a “service provider” or “contractor” under the CCPA and a person that processes personal data on behalf of a Controller under another Applicable Data Protection Law.

**“Security Incident”** has the meaning given in Section 8 of the Agreement.

**“Subprocessor”** means a third party Camplicity appoints to process Customer Personal Data on Customer’s behalf. It does not include a third-party service Customer independently directs Camplicity to connect to, such as Customer’s CampMinder account, for that third party’s independent processing.

## 2. Scope, roles, and instructions

Customer is the Controller and Camplicity is the Processor of Customer Personal Data unless Applicable Data Protection Law assigns the parties different terms with equivalent roles.

Camplicity will process Customer Personal Data only:

1. to provide, secure, support, and maintain the Services;
2. on Customer’s documented instructions in the Agreement, an order form, Customer’s configuration and use of the Services, or another written instruction consistent with the Agreement;
3. to prevent fraud, abuse, or Security Incidents; and
4. as required by applicable law.

If law requires processing beyond Customer’s instructions, Camplicity will notify Customer before processing unless law prohibits notice. Camplicity will tell Customer if, in our reasonable opinion, an instruction violates Applicable Data Protection Law. Camplicity need not follow an unlawful instruction and may suspend the affected processing while the parties seek a lawful alternative.

The processing details are described in Annex A.

## 3. Customer responsibilities

Customer is responsible for:

- complying with law in its collection, instructions, disclosures, and use of Customer Personal Data;
- providing legally required notices and obtaining required rights, authorizations, and consents, including for minors, staff, photos, and sensitive information;
- limiting Customer Personal Data to information reasonably necessary for camp operations and the Services;
- configuring roles, permissions, integrations, and Authorized Users appropriately;
- responding to Data Subjects and regulators concerning Customer’s processing; and
- avoiding prohibited data identified in the Agreement.

Customer will not instruct Camplicity to process personal information governed by the GDPR, UK GDPR, HIPAA, FERPA, or another regime requiring terms not included in this DPA unless the parties first sign an appropriate addendum.

## 4. Processor and service-provider restrictions

Camplicity will:

- process Customer Personal Data only for the limited and specified purposes described in the Agreement and this DPA;
- not sell Customer Personal Data or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising;
- not use Customer Personal Data for targeted advertising;
- not retain, use, or disclose Customer Personal Data outside the direct business relationship with Customer or for a commercial purpose other than the business purposes specified in the Agreement, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law;
- not combine Customer Personal Data with personal information received from another person or collected through Camplicity’s independent interactions with a Data Subject, except as permitted by Applicable Data Protection Law;
- provide the same level of privacy protection required of processors or service providers under Applicable Data Protection Law;
- notify Customer if we determine we can no longer meet a material requirement of Applicable Data Protection Law applicable to our processing; and
- allow Customer to take reasonable and appropriate steps to help ensure that processing is consistent with Customer’s obligations and to stop and remediate unauthorized processing, subject to Sections 9 and 13.

Camplicity may create aggregated or de-identified information as permitted by the Agreement. We will maintain measures designed to prevent the information from being associated with Customer or an individual and will not attempt to re-identify it except to test and maintain de-identification.

## 5. Confidentiality

Camplicity will ensure that personnel authorized to process Customer Personal Data are subject to confidentiality obligations and receive access only as reasonably necessary for their responsibilities. The Agreement’s confidentiality section also applies.

## 6. Security

Camplicity will maintain administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect Customer Personal Data against unauthorized access, acquisition, use, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. The current categories of safeguards are described in Annex B.

Customer understands that security measures evolve with technical developments, cost, risk, and changes to the Services. Camplicity may update Annex B or replace a safeguard with one that provides materially equivalent protection. This DPA does not promise a specific certification, audit report, penetration-testing frequency, uptime commitment, recovery objective, or feature not expressly stated in Annex B.

## 7. Subprocessors

Customer generally authorizes Camplicity to use the Subprocessors listed in Annex C. Camplicity will enter a written agreement with each Subprocessor requiring data-protection obligations appropriate to the processing and will remain responsible for the Subprocessor’s performance of Camplicity’s obligations under this DPA, subject to the Agreement’s limitations of liability.

Camplicity may update Annex C. Before a new Subprocessor begins materially processing Customer Personal Data, we will provide notice through an updated published list, email to Customer’s administrator, or in-product notice where reasonably practicable. Customer may object within 15 days after notice on reasonable, documented data-protection grounds.

The parties will work in good faith on a commercially reasonable solution. If none is available, Camplicity may choose not to use the Subprocessor for Customer, or Customer may terminate only the affected Services and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid unused fees for them. An objection does not relieve Customer of payment obligations for unaffected Services.

## 8. Data Subject requests

If Camplicity receives a request from a Data Subject concerning Customer Personal Data, we will direct the requester to Customer unless law prohibits doing so. Taking into account the nature of processing and functionality available in the Services, Camplicity will reasonably assist Customer with requests to access, correct, delete, or obtain Customer Personal Data.

Customer remains responsible for verifying requests, deciding whether a request is valid, communicating with the requester, and using available product functionality. If a request requires material engineering or manual work beyond ordinary assistance, Camplicity may charge a reasonable fee disclosed in advance unless Applicable Data Protection Law prohibits a fee.

## 9. Compliance assistance and review

Taking into account the nature of processing and information available to us, Camplicity will reasonably assist Customer with data-protection assessments, regulator inquiries, and consultations required by Applicable Data Protection Law and directly related to the Services.

On reasonable written request, Camplicity will provide information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. No more than once per year, Customer may submit a reasonable written security or privacy questionnaire. Additional requests are permitted after a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data or where a regulator or Applicable Data Protection Law requires them.

An on-site or third-party audit is available only if information supplied by Camplicity is insufficient to meet a legal audit requirement. It must occur during normal business hours, on reasonable advance notice, without access to another customer’s data, and under confidentiality and security restrictions. The auditor must be independent and not a Camplicity competitor. Customer pays its audit costs and Camplicity’s reasonable costs unless the audit identifies Camplicity’s material breach of this DPA.

Nothing in this section requires Camplicity to disclose another customer’s information, penetration-test details that would create a security risk, legally privileged material, or trade secrets unrelated to the processing.

## 10. Security Incidents

Camplicity will notify Customer without undue delay after determining that a Security Incident affecting Customer Personal Data occurred. Notice will be sent to Customer’s then-current administrator email unless Customer designates another security contact in writing.

As information becomes reasonably available, notice will describe:

- the nature of the Security Incident;
- the categories of Customer Personal Data and Data Subjects affected;
- known or reasonably estimated consequences;
- containment and remediation measures taken or planned; and
- a contact for follow-up.

Camplicity may provide information in phases and will take reasonable steps to investigate, contain, and remediate the Security Incident. We will reasonably assist Customer with legally required notices and regulator communications. Customer is responsible for deciding whether and how to notify Data Subjects, customers, insurers, or regulators unless law requires Camplicity to notify them directly.

Notification or cooperation is not an admission of fault or liability. Unsuccessful access attempts, scans, denial-of-service attempts, and events that do not compromise Customer Personal Data are not Security Incidents.

## 11. Government and legal requests

If Camplicity receives a legally binding request for Customer Personal Data, we will, to the extent legally permitted:

1. notify Customer before disclosure;
2. direct the requesting authority to Customer where reasonable;
3. disclose only the information legally required; and
4. reasonably assist Customer if Customer seeks a protective order, at Customer’s expense.

## 12. Return and deletion

Customer may use available product exports while access is active. If Customer requests an export within 30 days after access ends, Camplicity will provide the one-time standard export described in Section 10 of the Agreement.

No earlier than the end of that request period, unless Customer requests earlier deletion, and within 60 days after access ends, Camplicity will delete Customer Personal Data from active production systems or render it permanently de-identified, except information retained as required by law. Customer Personal Data may remain in access-restricted backups until overwritten or deleted through the ordinary backup lifecycle. Retained information remains subject to this DPA and will not be used for another purpose.

At Customer’s reasonable request, Camplicity will confirm completion of the active-system deletion process. The Agreement governs the format, scope, cost, and availability of exports.

## 13. Liability

The exclusions and limitations of liability in the Agreement apply to this DPA and all claims arising from it. This DPA does not create a separate or additional liability cap. The Agreement’s Security Incident and Customer Data confidentiality super-cap applies where its terms state.

## 14. Term and changes

This DPA begins when Customer accepts the Agreement and continues while Camplicity processes Customer Personal Data. Sections that protect retained Customer Personal Data continue for as long as Camplicity retains it.

Camplicity may update this DPA as described in the Agreement. A material reduction in our data-protection obligations will not apply during Customer’s then-current subscription term unless required by law or Customer agrees.

## 15. United States scope

This DPA is designed for processing governed by United States law. It does not include Standard Contractual Clauses, a UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or terms for international data transfers. Customer must contact contact@camplicity.app before using the Services for personal data governed by the GDPR, UK GDPR, or another international transfer regime.

# Annex A — Processing details

## A.1 Subject matter and duration

Camplicity processes Customer Personal Data to provide, host, secure, support, and maintain the Services during the Agreement and the limited export, deletion, backup, and legal-retention periods afterward.

## A.2 Nature and purposes

Processing may include receiving, importing, organizing, storing, displaying, searching, scheduling, assigning, analyzing, exporting, backing up, securing, troubleshooting, deleting, and de-identifying Customer Personal Data. At Customer’s direction, it may include synchronizing a connected service or processing an Authorized User’s prompt through an available AI feature.

## A.3 Data Subjects

- campers, including minors;
- prospective or former campers entered by Customer;
- Customer employees, staff members, contractors, and volunteers;
- parents or guardians if Customer places their information in free-text or imported data;
- Authorized Users and customer administrators; and
- other individuals Customer includes in camp operational records.

## A.4 Categories of Customer Personal Data

- names, dates of birth, gender, staff phone numbers, photos, descriptions, and notes;
- group, session, housing, activity, trip, meal, elective, availability, certification, and scheduling information;
- allergy and dietary labels and other sensitive information Customer chooses to enter;
- customer-created tags, free-text records, and imported fields;
- external-system identifiers and integration-related records;
- user identity, role, access scope, and activity records;
- audit, authentication, import-job, support, AI conversation, and security records; and
- other Customer Data submitted under the Agreement.

The Services are not intended for Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, payment-card numbers, detailed medical records, medication administration, or data regulated by HIPAA or FERPA without a separate written agreement.

## A.5 Processing frequency

Continuous or as initiated by Customer and its Authorized Users during the subscription, followed by limited export, deletion, backup, and legal-retention processing.

# Annex B — Technical and organizational measures

The measures below describe current control categories. They apply where relevant to the feature and production environment and do not represent a certification or guarantee that an incident cannot occur.

## B.1 Infrastructure and transmission

- Primary production application infrastructure is hosted in Amazon Web Services in the United States, principally the `us-east-1` region.
- Production application traffic uses HTTPS.
- Camper and staff photos are stored in a private Amazon S3 bucket configured to block public access, deny insecure transport, and use server-side encryption. Access is provided through time-limited signed URLs.

## B.2 Identity and access

- Authorized Users authenticate through Auth0.
- The API validates signed authentication tokens, issuer, and audience.
- Application permissions use role-based authorization and scoped access rules.
- Customer records are scoped by tenant and camp in the application and database access layer.
- Inactive users and tenants are blocked from ordinary authenticated access.
- Customer administrators control user authorization and role assignment, subject to product functionality.

## B.3 Secrets and integrations

- Application secrets are supplied through restricted environment configuration and production secret-management services.
- Stored CampMinder and similar integration credentials are encrypted using AES-256-GCM.
- Payment-card information is processed by Stripe; Camplicity does not store complete card numbers.
- Stripe webhook signatures are verified before event processing.

## B.4 Application and operational controls

- The Services apply request validation and feature-specific rate limits.
- Customer-directed changes to supported entities are recorded in camp-scoped audit logs that include actor, action, entity, source, and timestamp information.
- Import-job files and records are subject to cleanup periods, ordinarily 30 days for successful jobs and 90 days for failed jobs.
- A health endpoint checks application and database availability for infrastructure health checks.
- Development and production configurations are separated through environment configuration.

## B.5 Security response

- Camplicity maintains channels for reporting suspected security issues.
- Camplicity investigates reported or detected events and takes reasonable containment and remediation measures appropriate to the event.
- Security Incident notification and cooperation follow Section 10 of this DPA.

## B.6 Customer responsibilities

The effectiveness of these measures depends on Customer securing its devices and networks, maintaining current administrators, removing users who no longer need access, assigning least-privilege roles, protecting exports, reviewing AI output, and promptly reporting suspected compromise.
